Most Indian states revise minimum wages twice a year, from 1 April and 1 October, through a variable dearness allowance (VDA) linked to the consumer price index. The October revision is usually notified late. Several of the October 2026 orders were issued in mid or late October and apply backwards to 1 October, so October salaries processed before the order came out may need arrears.

This guide lists the rates from 1 October 2026 for the states and the central sphere whose orders are out, explains how the VDA moved them, and sets out what to check in payroll.

## States on other cycles

Not every state revises in October, so a revision calendar built only on April and October misses several.

- **Maharashtra and West Bengal** revise on 1 January and 1 July.
- **Tamil Nadu** adjusts the dearness allowance once a year, from 1 April.
- **Punjab** revised its basic rates from 1 May 2026 under the Code on Wages: Rs. 13,486 a month for unskilled work.
- **Bihar** set rates from 1 April 2026 by S.O. 92 of 6 April 2026: Rs. 436 a day, or Rs. 11,336 a month, for unskilled work. The order provides for the next VDA change three months after each half-year.
- **Telangana** fixed new rates from 1 June 2026 under G.O.Ms.No.6.
- **Karnataka** notified its revision on 22 May 2026; it is under challenge in the High Court but has not been stayed.

## How VDA moves the rate

In a basic plus VDA system the basic wage stays fixed for years and the VDA changes every six months with the consumer price index. The order compares the average index for a six-month period with the index at which the basic wage was fixed, and adds the difference as VDA. Jharkhand's 11.65 per cent and Uttar Pradesh's (431 - 400) / 400 formula are two ways of writing the same idea.

Two consequences matter for payroll. First, the rate changes even when nobody announces a "wage revision", so the half-yearly VDA order is the document to watch. Second, a fall in the index does not always cut the rate: the Bihar and Jharkhand orders, for example, say the VDA does not go below the level already fixed.

## What to do in payroll

1. **Map each establishment to the right zone or area.** Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh and the central sphere all have more than one rate for the same grade.
2. **Check every grade, not only unskilled.** In Jharkhand the gap between semi-skilled and skilled Area A is more than Rs. 4,500 a month.
3. **Pay arrears from 1 October 2026** where October salaries used the April rate. Orders issued after the payroll cut-off still apply from 1 October.
4. **Compare the right figure.** Minimum wage is compared with the wages the order defines, usually basic plus dearness allowance. Allowances such as HRA do not count towards it.
5. **Keep the order with the payroll records.** An inspector will ask for the rate and its source together.

## Key takeaway

The October 2026 revisions are VDA increases on unchanged basic wages, and several were notified weeks after 1 October. Check each state's order, apply it from 1 October with arrears, and keep the states on January, April, May and June cycles on their own calendar.
